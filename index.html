Privacy Policy

Dr. Samskriti Koirala

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information submitted through our Facebook and Instagram advertisements and enquiry forms is collected, used and protected.

Dr. Samskriti Koirala is a dental professional providing dental consultations and treatment services at an associated dental clinic in Kathmandu Valley.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or request a consultation, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Contact phone number
  • General residential location or area
  • Dental service or consultation you are interested in
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Information about your dental concerns that you voluntarily provide

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to your dental enquiries.
  • Understand your consultation requirements.
  • Coordinate appointment requests with the associated clinic.
  • Communicate appointment availability and confirmation details.
  • Provide information about available dental services.

3. Sharing of Information

Information submitted through our advertisements may be shared with authorised personnel at the associated dental clinic for the purpose of responding to enquiries and coordinating consultations.

We may also use third-party platforms, including Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to receive and manage enquiries.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Protection and Retention

Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or to comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate details or deletion of information, subject to applicable legal and medical record-keeping requirements.

You may also request that we stop sending non-essential communications.

6. Children's Information

For enquiries concerning children, we ask that a parent or legal guardian provide the relevant information on the child's behalf.

7. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
